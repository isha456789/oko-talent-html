The OKO Story
We empower recruitment entrepreneurs to build sustainable, scalable businesses through our proven franchise model and the backing of industry giants.
Three decades of recruitment excellence
Born from CNA Recruitment’s heritage, part of the Pertemps Network Group, OKO Talent represents a new generation of recruitment business ownership. Founded in 1993, our heritage brand has evolved to meet the changing needs of todays recruitment professionals. We recognised that experienced recruiters want independence without isolation, technology without complexity, and growth without unnecessary risk. Our founders have a proven track record of building and scaling specialist recruitment brands, now offering that experience to ambitious recruiters ready to own their success. OKO Talent combines entrepreneurial energy with corporate-level support — allowing you to focus on what you do best: recruiting exceptional talent.
